One 1024×1024 front page for Thursday 26 November 2026, with 38 stories that all happened, or were first reported, on that day. Thirteen scenes are drawn in code: three leads — the 100th Macy's parade postponed by the Thanksgiving storm, India marking 18 years since 26/11 and Constitution Day, and the Cairo fuel-tanker fire — plus ten cards from Ras Tanura to Mercury. Every story carries a confidence label (CONFIRMED / REPORTED / OFFICIAL / CLAIM / ONGOING) and its named sources, so each claim can be checked on its own. A six-event UTC timeline, a world map with numbered pins 1–13, an index continuing 14–38 with a source per item, a ten-item date check of older stories left out, and a BY THE NUMBERS strip complete the page. The picture is drawn with PIL during the build — no photographs, no copied art.

## How to run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` checks the picture against the contest caps (a whole PNG, at most 1 MiB, at most 1024×1024) and copies it to the output directory. It writes identical bytes on every run.
